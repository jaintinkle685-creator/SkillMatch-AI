SAMPLE STUDENT ACCOUNT FIX

Fixed the sample-student loading issue for registered faculty/admin accounts.

Cause: the demo sample students used fixed database IDs. When a logged-in
registered account tried to load them, those IDs could already belong to the
demo account, so the database rejected the insert/update and the cohort stayed
empty.

Fix: sample students now receive deterministic account-scoped IDs before being
persisted. Demo and every registered faculty/admin account can therefore load
the full sample cohort independently and persist it across navigation/reload.
No other project behavior was intentionally changed.
